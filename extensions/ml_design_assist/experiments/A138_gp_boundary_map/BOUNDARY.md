# A138 - GP level-set active learning of the adopted design's 200 A line-step boundary under L / Cs tolerance (BOUNDARY)
Extension ml_design_assist (scope extended by the user, 2026-10-05). Written and committed before any campaign run.
Looked at beforehand: D63's prior on the candidate grid (a138_prior_grid.npz), a dry run of the loop with fake cosim,
four smoke runs at the L / Cs corners (tmp, not data: +8 V / 1 us 199-215 A, steady 144 A, timed 507-814 us).
Decision it changes: the bus-slew spec of the adopted single-module design (A130 measured it at nominal L / Cs only:
<= 4.8 V at >= 1 us, -8 V >= 6 us, +8 V >= 10 us) - whether L x 0.7-1.3 and Cs x 0.7-1.3 tighten it, and where.
Cheaper check done first: D63 + the RTL-exact cap (A136's VffLP, MAE 3.8 A / max +12 A on A136's 43 rows) over the grid.
Budget: <= 400 single-module cosim runs (smoke: 150-164 s each at 4 jobs; ~3 h at 10 jobs), one self-driving script.
Records: the raw run files (~5 MB each) stay local (.gitignore here); cfgs, a138_state.json (every run's response and
flags) and the summary are committed; the cfgs regenerate the records. The prior grid is regenerated by --prior.

## 1. What and why
- Design: A129's g125 + vff {rel_q8 320, rel_lp 1, seed 2} (C10's single-module cfg, = A137's s100 rows). Coss, the
  load (1000 A) and Vin 48 V nominal. One line step at 1000 us (phase 1 timed from 507-813 us), end 1200 us.
- Inputs per direction (rising, falling; one GP each): L x [0.7, 1.3], Cs x [0.7, 1.3] (Cs = 6 uF: MLCC DC-bias and
  tolerance), |dv| [4.8, 8.0] V, slew [1, 20] us (log). Response: the post-step peak (max high-side turn-off current at
  t >= 1000 us), clipped at 260 A; start-up peaks are not the response (mode S's, the depth-loop line).
- Model: y = D63 prior + GP residual (squared exponential, ARD, noise; ml_gp). The step's position in the cycle (2-7 A,
  A129) is the GP's noise.
- Data: per direction 20 Latin-hypercube points (training), 15 uniform random points (held-out test), A137's six
  rising runs (L x 0.7 / 1.0 / 1.3, +4.8 V / 1 and 5 us; seed 1 = seed 2 bit for bit, C10) as training data.
- Active learning: straddle 1.96 sd - |mean - 200| on the candidate grid (13 L x 13 Cs x 9 |dv| x 12 slews per
  direction), 10 per batch over both directions, kriging believer within a batch; stop at the budget or when < 1 % of
  either grid stays ambiguous for two batches.
- Spec table: for L, Cs in {0.7, 1.0, 1.3} and |dv| in {4.8, 6.4, 8.0} per direction, the smallest grid slew from which
  every slower grid slew has mean + 1.645 sd (with noise) <= 200 A. 20 cells drawn at random are run at that slew.
- Not tested: Coss tolerance, load steps at the corners, four modules, start-up (mode S), slews outside 1-20 us.

## 2. Criteria
1. Validity: every campaign run completes, its step lands in the timed mode, sources unmodified (else listed, excluded).
2. Held-out test (15 per direction, final GP): 90 % predictive intervals cover >= 80 %; the 200 A classification is
   right on >= 90 % and at least as often as the D63 prior's.
3. Verification: at least 18 of the 20 spec cells <= 200 A at their table slew, none above 205 A.
Reported: the same test numbers for the GP on the initial design only (what active learning added); length scales.

## 3. Predictions (D63 prior; the GP's residual corrects them)
- Rising: no |dv| = 4.8 V cell above 200 A at any slew (prior 168-195 A); +8 V needs >= 1.7-3.9 us at L x 1.3 and is
  never safe at L x 0.7 (peaks grow with slower slew: 200 -> 215 A at Cs x 0.7, 217 -> 231 A at Cs x 1.3) - a
  non-monotone slew dependence. Nominal +8 V: 2.3 us (A130 / A136 cosim: ~10 us; D63 under-reads rising steps).
- Falling: 4.8 V safe at 1 us everywhere; -8 V needs 2.3-8.8 us, the most at L x 0.7, Cs x 1.3 (prior diverges at
  1 us there).
- Cosim above the prior on rising rows (A136: mean +2.2 A, up to +12 A), so the table's rising slews will be longer.
- Test accuracy of the prior 80-90 % (rising cells near 200 A).

## 4. Decision rule
- 1-3 pass: the table is the adopted design's bus-slew spec under L / Cs tolerance (MULTI_MODULE_SUMMARY / CURRENT_STATUS
  spec item, scorecard T18); cells tighter than A130's spec are named.
- 2 fails: the GP's bands are not trusted; the table is reported with the misses, the verification runs stand alone.
- 3 fails: the spec cells that failed are tightened to the next slower grid slew that passed, or marked open.
- A non-monotone slew dependence confirmed in cosim is reported as a finding on its own (a minimum slew is not a spec).
