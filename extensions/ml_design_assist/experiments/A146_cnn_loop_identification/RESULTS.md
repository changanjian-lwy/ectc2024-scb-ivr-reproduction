# A146 - CNN identification of the commutation loop from one V_DS capture (RESULTS)
Boundary: 4e94e0b. Outputs: a146_summary.json (a146_run.py), a146_cnn.npz (network, scaler, conformal widths),
a146_posthoc.json (a146_posthoc.py). Traces: tmp/a146/traces.npz, regenerated by a146_data.py (seed 146, 1308 s).

## 0. Verdict
- **The CNN reads the loop off one capture.** Test medians (4000 captures, unknown probe 0.7-2 GHz, noise, jitter,
  I0 error): L 2.2 %, Q_act 4.0 %, di/dt 5.9 %, k 0.012, in 0.22 ms per capture. Its 90 % conformal intervals cover
  0.90 / 0.90 / 0.91 / 0.90.
- **Against the alternatives:**
  - The damped-sine fit (what one does with a scope): 7.9 / 25 / 42 %.
  - The 6-parameter simulator fit (trigger shift and probe bandwidth fitted too): 1.1 / 0.6 / 1.7 % and k 0.011,
    near the Cramer-Rao bound, at ~15 s per capture.
- **Cross-validation, the useful finding:** the two routes fail in different places.
  - The fit is more accurate where it converges. On 6 of 40 captures it runs into its bounds (fc or shift) and also
    loses L, Q, k by 10-120 %. 5 of those 6 are fast edges, t_f 0.12-0.33 ns; the CNN stays at 0.1-7 % on them.
  - Started from the CNN's estimate, the fit recovers 4 of the 6 (L, Q, k within 0.1-3 %) and needs 22 % fewer
    evaluations overall (24 vs 31).
  - **The procedure is CNN, then fit.**
- **di/dt has a physical limit:** an edge faster than the probe's response is degenerate with the unknown bandwidth.
  CNN 4.3 % at t_f >= 0.5 ns, 15 % below. The fit keeps 28-62 % on 2 fast captures from either start.
- **Measurement requirements (criterion 5 rows):**
  - Noise <= ~0.4 V rms: at 0.8 V, Q coverage drops to 0.63 although the median error is only 7 %.
  - Probe >= 0.7 GHz and > 1 / t_f for di/dt: at 0.5 GHz, di/dt error 19 %, coverage 0.58.
  - I0 sensed within ~2 %: at 5 %, k's coverage is 0.24. k comes from the ring amplitude per amp.
  - Trigger jitter up to 150 ps is tolerated.

## 2. Decision and limits
- Rule for 4 (find the biased side): both are, in different places. The registered conclusion holds in the stronger
  form above (CNN first, then fit).
- Rule for 5: the three rows become measurement requirements. For Mihai, the loop values can be measured on hardware
  by one double-pulse capture per phase, with a probe of at least ~1 GHz, noise of at most ~0.4 V and current sensed
  within 2 %.
- Limits:
  - Synthetic data from the same plant model; a real loop with a split L or Cin ESL is out of the model.
  - The probe is a 2nd-order low-pass.
  - 40 simulator fits only.
  - Intervals are marginal (not per region) and are not valid outside the training draws.
